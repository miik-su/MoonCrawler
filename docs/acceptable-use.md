# Acceptable use

Use MoonCrawler only for resources you are authorized or otherwise permitted to
access. Respect Robots.txt, site terms, applicable privacy/data-protection and
copyright requirements. Robots permission is not access authorization or a data
reuse license. The operator must determine the applicable permissions and rules.

Do not bypass authentication, CAPTCHAs or access controls, launch attacks, or cause
denial of service. Keep host scope, concurrency and request rates appropriate to the
site. Protect collected data and credentials and define retention/deletion rules.
The bundled crawler does not provide authentication or anti-bot bypass facilities.

This operational guidance does not modify the software's Apache-2.0 license.
